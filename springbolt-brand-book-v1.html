Guidelines // Brand Voice07 / 25

Tone Spectrum

Voice stays constant. Tone flexes by audience and context.

IMAGE: Horizontal spectrum bar. Left = "Maximum Edge" (black/teal). Right = "Warm & Accountable" (orange). Five channel markers along the bar. Uses brand colors only.

Website & Marketing // 90% Direct / 10% Warm

Maximum edge. Provocative headlines, proof-stacked claims.

Case Studies // 70% Direct / 30% Authoritative

Challenge, Solution, Outcome, Why It Matters. Data speaks first.

Cold Outreach // 80% Direct / 20% Conversational

Peer-to-peer. Reads like someone who already did the homework.

Client Communication // 50% Direct / 50% Warm

Accountable, transparent. Edge softens. We own mistakes fast.

Social & Thought Leadership // 85% Provocative / 15% Educational

POV-driven. Opinions that stop the scroll.

Language Rules

What we say. What we never say. What we delete on sight.

✓ We Say

One integrated system.

98% inbox placement.

We audit your math.

We eliminated the salesperson tax.

Every leader is an owner.

Definite Offer.

280M+ record engine

End-to-end solutions.

Best-in-class deliverability.

Let's hop on a quick call.

Competitive pricing.

Passionate about what we do.

Schedule a discovery call.

Robust database capabilities.

Kill List

Corporate Fog

Synergy. Omnichannel. Solutions. Leverage. Holistic. Robust. Best-in-class. World-class. Seamless.

Hedge Language

Might. Perhaps. Potentially. We believe. In our opinion.

Fake Warmth

We'd love to. We're excited to. Don't hesitate to reach out. Happy to help.

AI Hype

Revolutionary. Groundbreaking. Game-changing. Disruptive (we show it, don't label it).

Brand Voice // Detail17 / 25

Headline Formulas

Six patterns anyone can use to write like Springbolt.

F1 // The Named Enemy

"The [$ Amount] [Negative Noun]" → The $25,000 Illusion. The Salesperson Tax.

F2 // The Blunt Contrast

"[What they have] vs. [What we deliver]" → 5 vendors vs. 1 system.

F3 // The Three-Punch

"[Proof]. [Proof]. [Proof]." → One system. 98% placement. Zero middlemen.

F4 // The Provocation

"[Industry does X]. We [opposite]." → They sell process. We ship outcomes.

F5 // The Definite Claim

"[Number] [outcome] in [timeframe]" → 1.5B emails inboxed in 2025.

F6 // The Reframe

"You call it [X]. We call it [Y]." → You call it coverage. We call it redundancy.

Photos20 / 25

Photography Style

What our images feel like. What they never look like.

✓
IMAGE: Real workspace. Person at a screen with dashboards. Natural light, slightly desaturated. "Founder working late" not "stock meeting."

✓ Authentic. Operational. Real.

✗
IMAGE: Generic stock of diverse people laughing around a whiteboard. Over-lit, staged. This is what we avoid.

✗ Staged. Corporate. Empty.

Photo Rules

We Use

Real team members. Actual workspaces. Screens with real data. Urban/architectural (Dallas, Austin). Dark, moody color grading. Shallow depth of field.

We Avoid

Handshakes. Laughing meeting rooms. White studio backdrops. Over-bright flat lighting. Clipart. Illustrated people. Conference selfies with lanyards.

Graphic Elements & Patterns

The visual system beyond the logo.

IMAGE: The quarter-circle arc element from Springbot decks. Teal at 15% opacity on black background. Used in upper corners of slides and marketing materials. Signature spatial device.
IMAGE: Teal-to-transparent gradient (#14B8A6 100% to 0%). Shown as a background overlay on a dark section. This is the primary gradient from the style guide.

Graphic Devices

The Arc: Large quarter-circle. Teal or white at 10-20% opacity. Upper corners of layouts.

Teal Gradient: #14B8A6 at 100% fading to 0%. Used for hero overlays, backgrounds, and depth.

Sub-Brand Color Bar: Three-segment horizontal bar (green/teal/lavender) used as a divider showing the three engines.

"Without / With" Split: Side-by-side comparison. Left = pain (warm tones). Right = Springbolt (teal tones).

Stat Callouts: Large numbers in Barlow Black (teal) with small Barlow Regular label below.

Icons22 / 25

Iconography System

Consistent, functional icons across all touchpoints.

IMAGE: Grid of 12-16 line icons. 2px stroke, teal on white or white on black. Categories: Database, Email/Delivery, Activation, Innovation, Growth, Integration, Security, Speed, CRM, Analytics, US-Based, Ownership. 24x24px base grid. Rounded caps.

Icon Rules

Style

Line icons, 2px stroke. Rounded caps/joins. Single color: teal, black, or white. 24x24px grid. No filled, no multicolor.

Usage

Label Triple Threat services. Feature lists. Comparison tables. Always pair with text. Never use alone as CTA.

Fallback: If custom icons aren't available, use Lucide Icons (lucide.dev) or Phosphor Icons. These match our line-weight style. Never Font Awesome filled or emoji.

Charts23 / 25

Data Visualization

Numbers are our vocabulary. Here's how we show them.

IMAGE: Bar chart. "Industry Avg" (gray bar, 83%) vs. "Springbolt" (teal bar, 98%). Minimal axes. Large value labels on bars. White background. Barlow font.
IMAGE: Stat callout row. Three large numbers: "$40B" / "1.5B" / "98%" in Barlow Black, teal. Small labels below in Barlow Regular. White or black background.

Chart Rules

Simplify. If a chart needs more than 3 legend items, split it.

Label directly. Values on data points, not in separate legends.

Brand colors only. Teal = Springbolt. Gray = competitors. Orange = secondary.

Stat callouts over charts. When one number tells the story, use a big number, not a chart.
