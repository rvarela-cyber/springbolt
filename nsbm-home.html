A Scalable, Repeatable System.

Our first group of acquisitions brought together four companies with strong revenue and valuable data assets that were carrying too much cost. We consolidated them onto a single operating infrastructure and went to work.

An AI-augmented workforce took on the supporting roles. The aging infrastructure and codebase were modernized, then reconnected to a delivery process that ships products tied to revenue. Operating costs fell 75 percent, and the businesses returned to profit within the first quarter.

Ten months in, the group was self-sustaining, with 83 percent of its revenue recurring and combined growth and profit margins nearly double the benchmark investors use to judge a healthy company.
