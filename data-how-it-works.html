The Long Version

What happens inside each step.

The program is designed for busy teams. We handle coordination, preparation, and program management so your team participates with minimal disruption to day-to-day operations.

[ 01 ]

Qualification

You answer a short set of questions about your company: headcount, years in business, industry, and the tools your team works in. That’s it. We’re checking for the profile buyers are actively licensing:

  • 30 to 200 employees with real operational maturity
  • Significant internal documentation and process knowledge
  • Active use of modern software tools
  • Well-defined workflows your employees execute repeatedly

If you don’t fit, we tell you before you spend another minute on it.

[ 02 ]

The quick call

About ten minutes. We confirm the basics, answer your questions, and walk through what the rest of the process looks like for a company your size. Bring your skepticism. The people who ask the hardest questions on this call tend to become the happiest participants, because every answer holds up.

[ 03 ]

Counting your data

Together we go tool by tool through your stack and count what you have, about fifteen minutes per session. Nothing gets copied, uploaded, or accessed. You’re reading numbers off your own admin screens.

[ 04 ]

Your offer, your counsel, your signature

A real offer from a named U.S. buyer, based on the total volume and quality of your data. Compensation is set by dataset volume, workflow complexity, uniqueness, quality, and domain expertise. We always recommend your counsel review the agreement, and we won’t move a deal forward without it.

[ 05 ]

Transfer, scrubbing, and the wire

Once you sign, we take it from there. The transfer is handled securely, sensitive information is scrubbed, originals are deleted after processing, and you’re paid by wire. Nothing comes out of your check.

[ 06 ]

After the deal

You keep every original and keep operating exactly as you do today. You licensed a copy, like renting out a room instead of selling the house. The only thing that changed is your bank balance, and as new data piles up, there may be another deal down the road.
