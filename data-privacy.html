Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026. This policy covers this website and how we handle the information you share with us. It’s written to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, ask us.

[ 01 ]

Who we are

The Monetize Your Data program is operated by Springbolt Group (NSB Marketing, Inc.), 6060 N Central Expressway, Suite 500, Dallas, TX 75206. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page or by phone at 469-264-0370.

[ 02 ]

What we collect on this site

  • Information you give us. When you submit the qualification form we collect your name, role, company, website, work email, phone number, company size, industry, and anything you write in the message field
  • Usage information. Like most websites, we may collect standard technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited, and referring pages, through cookies or similar technologies

The qualification calculator runs in your browser. Your answers there aren’t transmitted or stored unless you submit the contact form.

[ 03 ]

How we use it

  • To evaluate whether your company qualifies for the program and to respond to you
  • To coordinate the program with our AI data lab when you ask us to move forward
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website
  • To meet legal and contractual obligations

We do not sell the personal information you submit on this site, and we don’t use it for third-party advertising.

[ 04 ]

Who we share it with

  • Our AI data lab, when you pursue the program, to run qualification, counting, and the offer process
  • Service providers that help us run the site and our business, under confidentiality obligations
  • Legal recipients, if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
[ 05 ]

Program datasets are different

This policy covers this website. Any company dataset licensed through the program is governed by a separate written agreement your counsel reviews before you sign. Under the program, sensitive information is scrubbed before any downstream use, customer information is never exposed, no data is publicly shared, original datasets are deleted after processing, companies retain ownership of their underlying data, and data is licensed only to U.S. based AI companies.

[ 06 ]

Retention and safeguards

We keep form submissions as long as needed to evaluate and support your participation, then for a reasonable period for business and legal records. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we hold. No website can promise absolute security, so we don’t.

[ 07 ]

Your choices and rights

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Opt out of further contact at any time

Depending on where you live, including under Texas and California law, you may have additional rights. Contact us and we’ll honor them. We don’t discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.

[ 08 ]

Other things worth stating plainly

  • This site is for businesses and isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information
  • If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the effective date at the top
  • Links to other websites are governed by those sites’ own policies
