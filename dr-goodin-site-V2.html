Common Questions

The questions high achievers actually ask.

I’m functioning well. Is therapy even for someone like me?
Yes, and you’re exactly who I work with. My clients are high functioning, intelligent, and capable. Many come to therapy to maximize their talents, understand themselves more deeply, and grow, at any age. You bring the ambition. I bring 24 years of insight into what ambition does to a person.
What’s a first session actually like?
A conversation with someone who gets it quickly. I listen for what sits beneath your words, and my clients often feel a sense of relief in the very first session, that moment of finally being understood. From there, we build the work together, as partners.
How private is this, really?
Completely. I run a private-pay practice, which means no insurance trail and no third parties in your business. Confidentiality is protected by law and by 24 years of professional practice. What you share stays between the two of us, with only the narrow legal exceptions every licensed psychologist must observe, which I’ll walk you through clearly.
Do you take insurance?
Sessions are private pay, which is exactly what most of my clients prefer. You keep full control of your privacy and your records. If you wish, I’ll provide documentation you can submit to your insurance company yourself.
How does teletherapy work with a packed schedule?
Sessions happen wherever you are in Florida, by video or by phone. Many of my clients prefer phone sessions, because voice-only removes every distraction and lets the work go deep. I’ve worked with clients remotely since 2012, long before it became the norm, and it fits a demanding calendar with ease.
Why work with someone this experienced instead of an app?
The big platforms will match you with whoever’s available. With me, you know exactly who you’re getting: a board certified psychologist with 24 years of experience, chosen by you, committed to you. When you’re trusting someone with the things you’ve never said out loud, who you get matters more than anything else.
Where are you licensed?
I’m licensed in the State of Florida and see clients throughout the state by teletherapy from my Palm Beach practice.
