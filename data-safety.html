Data Safety Is Everything

The number one question we get isn’t about money. It’s “is this safe?” Fair question. Here’s the complete answer: what gets removed, what the contract locks down, who can see what, and where your data can and cannot go. If any of it isn’t acceptable to you, the deal doesn’t happen.

The Principle

Protection isn’t a feature of this program. It’s the foundation.

Every dataset is handled under strict contractual protections. The purpose of the program is to help train and evaluate AI systems on realistic enterprise settings. It is expressly not the goal to replicate businesses, products, customers, or competitive advantages, and the contract says so in writing.

[ 01 ]

Nothing moves until you sign

Qualification, the call, and data measuring all happen with nothing copied, uploaded, or accessed. You’re reading numbers off your own admin screens. Data leaves your systems only after your counsel has reviewed the agreement and you’ve signed it.

[ 02 ]

What gets removed before anything is used

Before any downstream use, the dataset goes through scrubbing:

  • All personal info and identifying details are stripped, including names, emails, and phones
  • Customer information is never exposed
  • All confidential information is removed
  • Sensitive material is scrubbed before any downstream use
[ 03 ]

What the contract locks down

  • The buyer is named before you sign, so you know exactly where your data goes
  • Purpose is limited to training and evaluating AI systems
  • Replicating your business, products, customers, or competitive advantages is prohibited
  • No data is ever publicly shared
  • You retain full ownership of your underlying data
[ 04 ]

What happens to the originals

Original datasets are retained only for processing purposes, then deleted after processing. Nothing sits in a warehouse indefinitely, and nothing gets a second life you didn’t agree to. Your own originals stay with you the whole time.

[ 05 ]

U.S. buyers only. Period.

We license data to U.S. based AI companies only. We do not license data to Chinese companies or any foreign labs. The program runs with our AI data lab, an AI data company backed by Microsoft and trusted by leading U.S. AI companies.

[ 06 ]

Your lawyer has a seat at the table

Legal review is a built-in step, not an afterthought. Your counsel reads the agreement, the purpose limits, and your obligations before you sign. We won’t move a deal forward that your lawyer hasn’t seen, and we’ll walk through your own privacy policy and customer contracts with you so you can communicate responsibly about what you are and aren’t doing.

Under The Hood

Safety measures most people never think to ask about.

A few things worth knowing before you sign anything, anywhere.

[ 01 ]

The worst leaks are treated as the worst

Protection isn’t graded on a simple pass or fail. A missed bank account or government ID counts far more heavily against the process than a harmless date, so the scrubbing is held to its strictest standard exactly where exposure would hurt you most.

[ 02 ]

Disguised information can’t slip through

Personal details hiding behind typos, strange spacing, look-alike characters, hidden formatting, even encoded text: the process is stress-tested against deliberately hostile formatting, so information that a person scanning a file would never notice still gets caught and transformed.

[ 03 ]

No file can quietly go missing

Quality review starts from the list of everything that went in, not from what came out. A file that fails, times out, or gets skipped is caught and accounted for instead of silently dropping out of the audit. Human reviewers then compare samples against the originals.

[ 04 ]

One weak spot can’t hide behind a good average

The finished dataset is scored across five separate checks: privacy, usefulness, file integrity, completeness, and consistency. The scoring is built so excellence in one area can’t paper over a failure in another. If protection fails, the whole grade fails with it.
