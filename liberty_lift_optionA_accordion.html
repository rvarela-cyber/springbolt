Capillary Services

Capillary tubing delivers downhole treatment programs with pinpoint precision, ensuring 100 percent of the chemical reaches the correct point in the wellbore. This eliminates the uncertainty of annular backside treatments and the concern for annular pressures inhibiting delivery, giving operators full confidence that their chemical programs are working.

Coiled tubing photo

One call, we bring it all: sheaves, glycol, spools, tubing, check valves, mandrels, fittings, and tools. Everything the job needs is included in our setup.

  • Our capillary trailers are purpose-built for safety and efficiency, designed in collaboration with the manufacturer to exceed industry safety requirements. Every trailer is fully self-sufficient on location.
  • Self-loading and self-unloading capability, no additional equipment required on location
  • Triplex pump capable of pressure testing to 6,000 psi
  • Dedicated onboard hydraulic air compressor for pneumatic tools
  • Trailers can pump on the fly, both while pulling and running in hole
  • Hydraulically powered level wind keeps the operator out of the line of fire during pulling operations
  • Fully open spindle type secondary retention on sheaves, proof tested to 12,000 lbs
  • Sacrificial unions are the standard
  • Daily and monthly equipment inspections with documentation on every job

We carry competitively priced 1/4", 3/8", and 1/2" capillary tubing in 316L, 2205, 825, and 625 alloy compositions, with a minimum of 100,000' of 2205 stainless in inventory at all times. Custom pricing is available based on volume requirements.

  • 316L: Applicable for banded chemical delivery and surface applications; single-use downhole product
  • 2205: The Bakken standard; higher chromium and molybdenum content for superior corrosion resistance and strength; reusable downhole; typical inventory 100,000' to 300,000'
  • 825: Incorporates copper, titanium, and elevated nickel (38-46%); ideal for H2S scavenger delivery in moderate H2S environments with nickel-plated downhole equipment
  • 625: Minimum 58% nickel with elevated molybdenum and chromium; the most corrosion and temperature resistant option available; best suited for high H2S environments (Madison or Mission Canyon formations)

Liberty Lift conducts capillary tubing inspections using custom-built equipment, a service that has reduced customer capital expenditure on capillary tubing by up to 60 percent. Rather than replacing tubing on every run, we inspect, recertify and return it to service.

The inspection process includes
  • Pressure testing
  • Corrosion and splice removal
  • Tubing replacement or addition as needed
  • Recertification
  • Categorized storage at our yard until the customer is ready to redeploy
  • Full transparency of customer owned inventory with monthly rolling stock reports
