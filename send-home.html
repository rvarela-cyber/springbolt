CAN-SPAM is the US federal law governing commercial email. It requires every commercial email to include a physical mailing address, a working unsubscribe mechanism, non-deceptive subject lines, and clear sender identification. We require all clients to follow CAN-SPAM. Violations can carry fines of up to $43,792 per email, so we take compliance seriously on both sides.
